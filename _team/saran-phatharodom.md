Saran Phatharodom is a Ph.D. student in the ICE Lab at Drexel University. His work spans two
threads: the security of logic-locked circuits, where he models how hard a locked design is to
attack, and machine learning for physical design, where he contributes to graph-based timing
prediction and to the open datasets that make such models reproducible.

## Research interests

- Modeling and closed-form estimation of SAT-attack search complexity
- SAT-attack resilience measures for access-restricted circuits
- Graph representation learning for gate-level arrival time prediction, including long-tailed
  datasets
- Open schemas, datasets, and benchmarks for machine learning in EDA
