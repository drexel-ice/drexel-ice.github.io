Jeff Wu is a Ph.D. student whose primary research focus is on applying machine learning to analog circuit design and automation. He received his B.Sc in Electrical Engineering from University of Illinois in 2017 and his M.Sc in Electrical Engineering from University of Pennsylvania in 2019.

## Research interests

- Machine learning for analog circuit sizing, including classification-based formulations with
  adaptive labeling thresholds
- Variation-aware analog sizing using classifier chains
- Differentiable graph neural networks for wirelength estimation in physical design
- Machine learning and AI techniques for analog and RF EDA
