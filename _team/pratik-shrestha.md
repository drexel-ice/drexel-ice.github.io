Hello! I am a Ph.D. student in the ICE Lab, advised by Dr. Ioannis Savidis. I apply
representation learning to physical design automation: predicting late-stage design quality —
timing, parasitics, wirelength — from early design stages, and building the open data
infrastructure and benchmarks that make those results reproducible. I also work on hardware
security, where I developed a synthesis algorithm and security metric for finite-state-machine
locking. I received my B.E. in Computer Engineering from Kathmandu University in 2016.

## Projects

- **[EDA-Learn]({{ '/projects/eda-learn/' | relative_url }})** — an open research ecosystem for
  reproducible machine learning in EDA, spanning the
  [EDA-Schema]({{ '/eda-schema/' | relative_url }}) data model, datasets and benchmarks, and the
  [EDA-OpenML]({{ '/eda-openml/' | relative_url }}) model development framework.
- **[Hardware Security & Trust]({{ '/research/hardware-security/' | relative_url }})** —
  sequential logic locking and finite-state-machine obfuscation, including implementation on a
  65 nm technology node for tapeout.
