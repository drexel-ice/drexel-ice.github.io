Ziyi was a Ph.D. student in the ICE lab. Ziyi received his Bachelor degree from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Chengdu, in 2017 and received his Master degree from Northeastern University, Boston, in 2019.

## Research interests

- Synthesis of analog and mixed-signal circuits on reconfigurable and programmable arrays
  (FPAA)
- Analog obfuscation and hardware security built on reconfigurable analog arrays
- Side-channel attacks on analog circuits
- Post-fabrication tuning
- Machine learning and AI techniques for analog and RF EDA
