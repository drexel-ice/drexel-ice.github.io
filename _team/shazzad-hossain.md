Shazzad received the B.Sc. degree in electrical and electronic engineering from American International University Bangladesh in 2011. He received the M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in computer engineering from Drexel University in 2016 and 2021, respectively. He is now a Staff Engineer at Qualcomm. Before his Ph.D., he worked at Ulkasemi Private Limited in ASIC verification, physical design, PCB design, and IC layout. He was a Research Intern at Nokia Bell Labs in 2019, designing energy-efficient AI hardware accelerators.

## Research interests

- Ultra-low-voltage and near-threshold circuits and logic families
- Recycling and reuse of leakage current for energy-efficient multi-voltage systems
- Power management for MPSoC platforms, including dynamic idle-core management
- Energy-efficient near-memory computing and heterogeneous DNN accelerators
- Power distribution and clock delivery under ultra-low supply voltages
- Noise-constrained supply voltage selection for IoT applications
