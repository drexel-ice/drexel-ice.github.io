Alec Aversa received the B.Sc. degree in both computer science and mathematics with a minor in statistics from Misericordia University, Dallas, PA, USA, in 2020. He received the M.Sc. in computer engineering from Drexel University, Philadelphia, PA, USA, in 2023. He is currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Drexel University, where he conducts research in the Integrated Circuits and Electronics (ICE) Design and Analysis Laboratory.

## Research interests

- Open data models, datasets, and benchmarks for machine learning in physical design,
  through the [EDA-Schema]({{ '/eda-schema/' | relative_url }}) effort
- Design obfuscation and logic locking
- Security implications of 2.5-D and 3-D heterogeneous mixed-signal integration
- Academic reference design flows and shared research infrastructure, as a contributor to the
  IEEE CEDA DATC Robust Design Flow
