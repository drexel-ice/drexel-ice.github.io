Alec Aversa received the B.Sc. degree in both computer science and mathematics with a minor in statistics from Misericordia University, Dallas, PA, USA, in 2020. He received the M.Sc. in computer engineering from Drexel University, Philadelphia, PA, USA, in 2023. He is currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Drexel University, where he conducts research in the Integrated Circuits and Electronics (ICE) Design and Analysis Laboratory. His current research focuses are design obfuscation and logic locking, electronic design automation for machine learning and 3D circuits, and security implications of 2.5D and 3D heterogeneous mixed-signal circuits.
