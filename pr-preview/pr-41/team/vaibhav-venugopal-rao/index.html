Dr. Vaibhav Venugopal Rao

Staff Engineer, Kioxia | vaibhavvrao93@gmail.com

Vaibhav is a Research Engineer at Kioxia. Vaibhav received his M.Sc. and Ph.D. degree in computer engineering from Drexel University, PA, USA, in 2017 and 2024, respectively. His Ph.D. work focused on circuit level techniques to protect analog IP from intellectual property theft and counterfeiting. He has experience in designing analog and custom integrated circuits and in algorithm development for EDA tools.

Research interests

  • Protection of analog and mixed-signal IP through parameter and mesh-based obfuscation
  • Performance and security analysis of obfuscated analog circuits, and the cost borne by deobfuscation attacks
  • Satisfiability-modulo-theory–based search space exploration for security-oriented analog sizing
  • Variation-aware, multi-objective automation of analog transistor sizing to reduce design iterations
  • Security vulnerabilities introduced by 2.5-D and 3-D integration
