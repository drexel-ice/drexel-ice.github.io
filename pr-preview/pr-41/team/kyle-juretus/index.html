Prof. Kyle Juretus

Assistant Professor, Villanova University | kyle.juretus@villanova.edu

Kyle Juretus received his Ph.D. from Drexel University and is now an Assistant Professor at Villanova University. He was an NDSEG fellow and received Bachelor of Science degrees in Computer Engineering and Electrical Engineering from Drexel University in 2014.

Research interests

  • Gate-level logic encryption and locking with reduced area, power, and delay overhead
  • SAT-attack resilience, in-cone locking, and increased output corruption
  • Sequential logic locking through synthesized hidden state transitions
  • Key leakage through the scan chain, and its mitigation
  • Securing analog and mixed-signal circuits through shared dependencies
  • Hardware-based packet and preamble obfuscation for wireless communication, and physical-layer encryption for OFDM systems
  • Side-channel leakage, including machine learning on the thermal side channel
